Hi! Most of the interface of the skins creator is self-explanatory, but I made this to highlight some details.
Just in case...


Use the numbers below to follow along the left side of the numbered section of the provided html.


1-Images   You start by selecting an img of choice to overlay over the walkie/ham radio

2-Fit      Options are pretty straight-forward.

3-         Readability Toggles for various things. 

4-Name     This part is semi-important. Name your skin something you'll remember easily.
             -then from the drop-down choose which skin to replace. (there's 8 total)
			 
			 
5=Export   Most important steps--
             -I'd recommend using the button "Save straight to my Zomboid/Mods folder"
			  (this assuming the folder exists here at C:\Users\YOURPCNAME\Zomboid\mods) --usually default, refer to "4.png in this folder to see an an example alongside the PZ "example" mod lol.
			  
			  
			  **NOTE -if in single player- Nothing needs done here actually you can skip this step and go to step 6.
			 -Go into the folder that you've named your walkie/ham after. Find mod.info and open it.
			  - look for this id=RT_Skin_Bloody or id=YOURWALKIESKINNAME
			  
			   -if in single player-read above about skipping this section down to 6 silly.
			   -if in local hosted multiplayer-add this id to your servertest.ini after RadioTraders   "Mods=\RadioTraders;\RT_Skin_Bloody"   *make sure its loaded after*
			   -if in dedicated multiplayer-same as above for local hosted, needs added to your servertest.ini but not to workshop items like traditional mods since its loaded locally
			   
			   This setup keeps the files client side. You could share a skin with a friend if you provide them your local mod folder and they install it in their Zomboid/Mods.
			   
6 Lastly and critical. This mod and Radio Traders will need to be enabled in the main menu and then will require a full game restart to take effect. See png #2.



Thats it. Pretty easy. I'm easy to reach mostly. Give me a shout if you run into a problem after following this guide.
